# Dragos Crintea

## Overview
Dragos Crintea is the author of the eight-part semantic-layer-for-GenAI series this wiki is compiled
from, and the builder of [semantido](semantido.md).[^where-it-all-started-why-building-a-semantic-layer-for-genai] He describes a career spent as a
Principal / Solution Architect and (Senior) Data Engineer, and frames the series as applying good
engineering and system-architecture principles to the GenAI era.[^where-it-all-started-why-building-a-semantic-layer-for-genai]

## Key Facts
- **Type:** person
- **Background:** Principal / Solution Architect and (Senior) Data Engineer.[^where-it-all-started-why-building-a-semantic-layer-for-genai]
- Jumped onto the GenAI bandwagon in mid-2024 and invested ~GBP 15k in 2025 courses for direction.[^where-it-all-started-why-building-a-semantic-layer-for-genai]
- Built a gym-assistant vertical agent during the 2025 AI Makers bootcamp (Cohort 5), which exposed brittle Text2SQL and motivated semantido.[^where-it-all-started-why-building-a-semantic-layer-for-genai]
- Creator of [semantido](semantido.md) and operator of [Hikari Labs](hikari-labs.md)'s semantic-layer advisory.[^the-semantic-layer-landscape-what-you-need-to-know-before-choosing-one]
- Writes from an "AI realist" stance, arguing good engineering principles matter more in the GenAI era, not less.[^where-it-all-started-why-building-a-semantic-layer-for-genai]
- **Notable for:** the semantic-layer article series and semantido.[^semantics-where-the-code-lives-interchange-where-the-stack-lives-semantido-v0-3-0-release]
