# Hikari Labs

## Overview
Hikari Labs is the organisation behind [semantido](semantido.md): the open-source library lives under
its GitHub org (`github.com/hikarilabs/semantido`), and the company offers a semantic-layer advisory
to data and AI leadership teams.[^the-semantic-layer-landscape-what-you-need-to-know-before-choosing-one][^semantics-where-the-code-lives-interchange-where-the-stack-lives-semantido-v0-3-0-release]

## Key Facts
- **Type:** company
- Hosts the semantido open-source repository (`github.com/hikarilabs/semantido`).[^the-semantic-layer-landscape-what-you-need-to-know-before-choosing-one]
- Runs an advisory practice that helps data and AI leadership teams evaluate and implement semantic-layer architecture for GenAI applications.[^semantics-where-the-code-lives-interchange-where-the-stack-lives-semantido-v0-3-0-release]

- **Key people:** [Dragos Crintea](dragos-crintea.md).[^the-semantic-layer-landscape-what-you-need-to-know-before-choosing-one]
